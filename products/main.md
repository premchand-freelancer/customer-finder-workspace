- **Stable project key:** `creasto-web-development`
- **Primary service:** Premium website design and development for businesses seeking a new website or a redesign.
- **Secondary service:** AI-generated UGC video advertisements for brands. Search for these opportunities only on an explicit UGC request. Use a separate service profile and history key for that search.
- **Market:** CREASTO is based in India and serves businesses across India, including metropolitan cities, small towns and districts. Buyer language preferences are unknown.
- **Preferred buyers:** Owners and authorized representatives of small businesses, especially clinics, restaurants, gyms, salons, schools and similar local businesses. Other Indian businesses with an explicit website brief also qualify.
- **Business sources:** The owner supplied the service and market details above, the [official website](https://creasto.pages.dev/) and the [portfolio](https://premchandv.vercel.app/). Neither page could be verified on 2026-11-21. Specific technical capabilities, completed client projects, client identities, testimonials, prices and credentials are unknown.

## Default search

Treat “Find 10 new customers” as a request for up to ten new website design or development opportunities under this project key. Use reverse prospecting. Start from a buyer's public request and establish which business it represents. A missing or dated website, a directory entry or a vendor advertising its own services does not establish buying intent.

Prioritize recent owner requests for a new site or redesign, calls for proposals and businesses publicly hiring a website designer or developer for a project. For a hiring post, check whether the buyer accepts freelance, contract, studio or vendor proposals. Exclude employee-only vacancies from the customer shortlist.

## Evidence required for each opportunity

- Open the buyer's original public post, project brief or hiring page. Link it, identify the business and the requester's role where evidenced, and summarize what website work the buyer actually requested.
- Record the publication date (or `unknown`), date checked, any deadline and current status. Prioritize recent requests. An undated request needs other current evidence that proposals are still welcome. Exclude expired, filled, withdrawn and resolved requests.
- Verify and link a public business route the buyer invites for a proposal. Check that submission is completely free. Exclude required deposits, paid bids, application credits that must be purchased and mandatory paid memberships.
- Count an opportunity as **verified** only when the original source supports buying intent, the request is still open and a free proposal route is verified. Keep **uncertain** items outside the count and say which fact is missing. Exclude fake or speculative leads. Return fewer than requested when the evidence warrants it.
- Use intentionally public business information. Exclude private contact details, personal contact enrichment and brokered lists. Research and manual drafts confer no permission to send emails, messages or proposals, or to make purchases.
