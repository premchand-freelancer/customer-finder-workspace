# CREASTO customer profile

- **Stable project key:** `creasto-web-development`
- **Primary service:** Premium website design and development for businesses seeking a new website or a redesign.
- **Secondary service:** AI-generated UGC video advertisements for brands. Search for these opportunities only on an explicit UGC request. Use a separate service profile and the history key `creasto-ai-ugc` for that search. Apply the same zero-investment rule.
- **Market:** CREASTO is based in India and serves businesses across India, including metropolitan cities, small towns and districts. Buyer language preferences are unknown.
- **Preferred buyers:** Owners and authorized representatives of small businesses, especially clinics, restaurants, gyms, salons, schools and similar local businesses. Other Indian businesses with an explicit website brief also qualify.
- **Business sources:** The owner supplied the service and market details above, the [official website](https://creasto.pages.dev/) and the [portfolio](https://premchandv.vercel.app/). Neither page could be verified on 2026-11-21. Specific technical capabilities, completed client projects, client identities, testimonials, prices and credentials are unknown.

## Default search

Treat “Find 10 new customers” as a request for up to ten new website design or development opportunities under this project key. Use reverse prospecting. Start from a buyer's public request and establish which business it represents. A missing or dated website, a directory entry or a vendor advertising its own services does not establish buying intent.

Search India nationwide, including smaller cities and districts. Prioritize public LinkedIn posts requesting freelance website developers, genuine buyer requests in public Facebook groups, and client hiring posts in Reddit communities. Also prioritize business requests for web design proposals, agency calls for freelance subcontractors and other demonstrably free routes. For a hiring post, check whether the buyer accepts freelance, contract, studio or vendor proposals. Exclude employee-only vacancies from the customer shortlist.

## Zero-investment budget

CREASTO has $0 available to find or apply for projects. Use free discovery and proposal routes only. Exclude Freelancer.com projects requiring a minimum wallet balance and Upwork opportunities requiring purchased Connects. Exclude paid bidding, application credits, required subscriptions or memberships, deposits, wallet recharges, paid verification and any other mandatory upfront payment. Never assume credits, wallet funds or premium access are available. Exclude unpaid or speculative work, employee-only roles, expired opportunities and unverified requests. Respect platform rules, group rules and the buyer's allowed contact methods.

## Evidence required for each opportunity

- Open the buyer's original public post, project brief or hiring page. Link it, identify the business and the requester's role where evidenced, and summarize what website work the buyer actually requested.
- Record the publication date (or `unknown`), date checked, any deadline and current status. Prioritize recent requests. An undated request needs other current evidence that proposals are still welcome. Exclude expired, filled, withdrawn and resolved requests.
- Record the exact application or reply link, the contact method the buyer allows, and a dated check of that route. Cite the public invitation or official terms and explain how they establish that the entire submission is free. Keep the evidence in the local report.
- Count an opportunity as **verified** only when the original source supports buying intent, the request is still open for paid work, freelancers or agencies may apply, and the specified proposal route is permitted and demonstrably free of any upfront payment or application credit. Keep **uncertain** items outside the count and say which fact is missing. Exclude fake or speculative leads. Return fewer than requested when the evidence warrants it.
- Use intentionally public business information. Exclude private contact details, personal contact enrichment and brokered lists. Research and manual drafts confer no permission to send emails, messages or proposals, or to make purchases.
